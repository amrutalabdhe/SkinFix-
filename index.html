Content guardrails

✓ Do's
  • Show all 3 steps of the routine entirely
  • Shoot in natural lighting — avoid an overly staged or filtered look
  • Show the cream's texture
  • Keep length between 15–45 seconds
  • Blend the cream fully into skin (not caked on)
  • Use an authentic, relatable tone
✕ Don'ts
  • Using filters that distort skin
  • Covering your face
  • Showing before/after without context or words
  • Exaggerating or fabricating results
  • Using copyrighted music or content
